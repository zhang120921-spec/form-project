## Nine-Hole Ratings

All nine-hole ratings (front_cr, back_cr, front_slope, back_slope) are derived from the 18-hole Course Rating divided by two, with the same Slope. These are marked as ESTIMATED until published nine-hole ratings are sourced for each course.

**Note:** Using estimated Course Ratings and Slopes will silently affect every rating derived from a round played on that course. Verified ratings are strongly preferred. If you have access to published Course Ratings for any of the estimated courses, please submit them to an administrator for verification.
